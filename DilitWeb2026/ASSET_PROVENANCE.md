## Prompt de la imagen editorial

> Create a photorealistic editorial photograph for a premium Colombian industrial printing-chemicals company website hero. Extreme close-up of a modern offset/lithographic printing press in operation: polished steel rollers, paper or flexible packaging substrate moving through the machine, a glimpse of cyan, magenta, and yellow ink on the printed material. Human-free, brand-free, no text, no labels, no bottles, no invented logos. Sophisticated technical realism, tactile machinery, dramatic directional daylight with deep ink-blue shadows, restrained color, crisp material detail. Wide landscape framing with the most interesting machine detail on the right and calmer dark negative space toward the left, suitable for a website crop. Avoid generic stock-photo lighting and futuristic sci-fi effects.
