# Dilit website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, as preferred in the project brief for GoDaddy Web Hosting Deluxe.

## Users
Printing companies, packaging manufacturers, industrial customers and distributors evaluating specialty chemical solutions.

## Product Purpose
Introduce Distribuidora Litográfica Dilit S.A.S., explain its Sellalit adhesive line and other products, and make direct contact easy.

## Operating Context
Dilit serves printing, packaging and graphic finishing industries in Colombia. The existing site states more than forty years developing specialized formulations, with technical service and personalized support.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Spanish-only single page. Preserve company, Sellalit, other products and contact order. Show all six Sellalit variants with their specified compatible materials. No database, WordPress, paid API or live-site deployment in this phase.

## Brand Commitments
Use the official Dilit logo without alteration. Its blue, yellow and magenta are identity cues. Use the original Sellalit container imagery without changing labels or packaging.

## Product Principles
- Company identity leads; Sellalit is the main product family.
- Product facts remain precise and attributable to supplied material or the existing site.
- Contact methods are direct and accessible.
