## Overview

**Creative North Star: The Course Index.** This is an editorial reading surface for six weeks of experiments. The interface gives each topic room to breathe and lets future artifacts, reflections, and embeds become the focus.

The existing portfolio contributes its airy blue, coral, yellow, and pink palette, open type, generous space, and quick underline behavior. Its composition is not repeated here.

## Colors

**The Coordinated Color Rule.** The page stays on warm off-white, with a plain section heading. Small week-number blocks cycle through blue, coral, yellow, and pink; soft matching washes appear on hover, focus within, or a linked target. Near-black ink carries the content and preserves contrast.

## Typography

The display title has a large, open sans-serif voice. Supporting copy stays in a comfortable reading measure. Tabular numerals keep the six-week sequence aligned.

## Layout

**The Growing Entry Rule.** Each weekly entry starts as a ruled row and grows vertically when a link, reflection, or embed is added. The page uses one wide container with a narrower introductory reading measure. At phone widths, content remains in one column with the number beside its heading.

Long titles may wrap within their column, including at enlarged text sizes; they must not create horizontal scrolling.

**The Equivalent Response Rule.** Each weekly heading is a real permalink with a 44px minimum touch target. Hover, keyboard focus within, and touch navigation to the linked target produce matching row emphasis. The browse link receives a fast coral underline fill on hover, focus, and press. Reduced-motion settings keep the color feedback and remove movement.

## Elevation & Depth

The site is flat. Fine rules separate content; shadows and decorative layering are absent.

## Shapes

The page uses straight edges and simple horizontal rules. No cards or rounded containers frame the weekly work.

## Components

- **Weekly entry:** two-digit week number in a color block, linked topic heading, status, then optional artifact content.
- **Text link:** underline in the reading flow, a coral underline fill on hover, and a strong visible focus outline.
- **Embed:** responsive 16:9 wrapper with a named iframe and a quiet blue fallback surface.
- **Footer credit:** a small, understated name below the weekly work.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep each week easy to scan and edit in place.
- Do let the artifacts supply future visual interest.
- Don't add decorative imagery, large scroll animations, or extra navigation to this Week 1 shell.
