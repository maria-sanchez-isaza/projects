# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML and CSS, as explicitly required by the brief. No build step.

## Users
Visitors to a course portfolio who want to find weekly learning artifacts and reflections.

## Product Purpose
This one-page portfolio introduces the Exploring eLearning Tools course work and collects one learning artifact per week over six weeks. A visitor should understand the purpose and find each week's work as it becomes available.

## Operating Context
Week 1 begins with a simple website shell. The same site will be updated throughout the course and published to GitHub Pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Six weekly entries, with Week 1 marked “Portfolio shell created” and Weeks 2–6 marked “Coming soon.”
- Future entries can hold artifact links, reflections, and iframe embeds.
- Self-contained folder with relative paths, no build or server requirement, and no analytics, forms, database, login, or unnecessary dependencies.
- Responsive, keyboard accessible, touch friendly, and motion considerate.

## Brand Commitments
- Course focused, with a small, understated credit to Maria Sanchez Isaza.
- Minimal, editorial, and personal.
- Existing portfolio is a stronger reference for its light blue, coral, yellow, pink, off-white, and black palette, plus hover behavior, subtle motion, and personality; its layout is not a template.

## Evidence on Hand
The course title, introduction, six weekly topics, and initial statuses were supplied in the brief. No weekly artifacts have been supplied yet.

## Product Principles
- Let weekly work become the focus as it is added.
- Make the six-week sequence immediately scannable.
- Keep updates simple enough to make directly in HTML.
- Use only content that has been supplied or later added by the author.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, semantic headings, visible focus, sufficient contrast, touch targets, reduced-motion support, descriptive links, and accessible future embeds are required. No information should depend on color or hover alone.
