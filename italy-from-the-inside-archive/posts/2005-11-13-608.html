Welcome to Italy From The Inside!

November 13, 2005 · General

Welcome to our blog Italy From The Inside. My name is Paolo Tosolini and with my wife Francesca have decided to start publishing a series of useful tips for travelers planning to visit Italy for the first time. Bookstores already offer a variety of travel guides about Italy, but they mostly focus on places to see and things to do. There is very little about introducing you to the real Italian culture, the way people do things in Italy, from driving to shopping to eating. And the more you'll learn about it before your trip, the better prepared you'll be to appreciate your sojourn. Please enjoy the reading and please send us your comments on specific topics you'd like us to cover.
