Watch out for that big P

December 7, 2005 · General

In Italy, you can start driving at 18. Students in the process of getting their license, can drive dad's car (with daddy inside) if they display a big letter 'P' on the back of the car. P stands for principiante, that is beginner. The journey to graduate to 'crazy Italian driver' implies the following next steps: - get the license - get rid of the un-cool P sticker - convince daddy that there is no need to come along anymore
