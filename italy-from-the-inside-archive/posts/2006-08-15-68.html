How much does gas cost in Italy?

August 15, 2006 · General

If you are planning to rent a car in Italy, you may wish to know how much gas costs these days. One gallon of normal gas (not premium) costs around $6.70. In our recent trip, we opted for a diesel rental car, which got quite a good mileage and for the same gallon of fuel we paid 'just' $5.78 ... sigh...
