Getting the best exchange for your money

May 9, 2007 · General

What is the best way to exchange your US dollars to Euros in Italy? The answer is simple: ATMs. Out of the several available options (exchange offices, traveler's checks, credit card withdrawals, etc), the most convenient and practical way remains to use your debit card in one of the many Bancomat machines (that's how ATMs are called in Italy). Although both yours and the Italian bank may charge a little fee, in our experience a few Euros fee is well worth your piece of mind to avoid carrying along large amount of cash all the time. That said, it's always a good idea to keep a safe amount of cash for emergencies, possibly in a hidden money belt like Rick Steves' silk one.
