Happy Birthday in Italian (video)

September 3, 2007 · Language, Media

My daughter Silvia has turned 7 and we decided to give her a nice present: a trip to Yosemite National Park to celebrate her birthday with her friend Amanda Maruzzi. Amanda lives in Milan and was visiting California with the family. For the past 6 consecutive years, the two little girls managed to celebrate this special day together. We decided the tradition should live on. In this video you'll learn how to sing Happy Birthday (Buon Compleanno) in Italian. Enjoy! - Can't see the video?
