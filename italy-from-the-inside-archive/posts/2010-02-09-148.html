There's always room for dessert

February 9, 2010 · Food

I recently went to a Pizzeria with some friends and finally, after years, I was able to enjoy one of my favorite desserts: the profiteroles. Profiteroles are small round puffs either filled with pastry cream or whipped cream, and covered with a chocolate sauce. Whipped cream is also used to add sweetness, decoration and calories... Don’t forget to order some next time you go to an Italian Pizzeria! P.S. In our eBook we devote an entire chapter to the Italian cuisine. Get the insider scoop!
