In Venice everything is art

April 29, 2010 · Places

What I’ve always loved about Venice is to get lost in its calli (narrow pedestrian streets) and find out unusual things on the way as a result. This happened also during our last trip to this magnificent city. I noticed many people going in and out this beautiful building. Curious, I went to check it out and realized that this was the City Hospital (Ospedale Civile). This great example of Renaissance architecture makes you think that in Venice everything, and I really mean everything, is art.
