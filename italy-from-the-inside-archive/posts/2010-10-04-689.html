Italian art masterpieces now available in super high definition

October 4, 2010 · General

Art lovers will enjoy the recent release of the Uffizi Gallery masterpieces as gigapixel photos fully accessible from your computer. A consortium of Italian and multinational brands has funded the project HaltaDefinizione which has captured super high resolution photos of some of the greatest treasures of Italian art. Images can be zoomed in to an impressive level of detail, enabling a new level of accessibility to a piece of Italian historical-artistic heritage.
