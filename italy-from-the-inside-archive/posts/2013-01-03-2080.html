January 3, 2013 · General, Media

We are happy to announce the release of our new Kindle eBook titled "Tips for Transportation: All You Need to Know to Get Around in Italy." Download this eBook on your iPhone, iPad or Kindle device and take it with you in your next trip to Italy.   • Airports
  • Buses
  • Car rentals
  • Ferries
  • Taxi cabs
  • Trains
Priced at an affordable $1.99, this eBook features more than 110+ Kindle pages, 50+ original photos and a glossary of 100+ useful Italian words. We welcome your comments as well as your Amazon reviews!
