The Italian piazza

May 27, 2014 · Culture, Places

Piazza Unità, Trieste Italy couldn't exist without the concept of piazza. If you leave behind its architectural definition, the true meaning of piazza is pure social life. Italians meet in the piazza, dance, chat, walk in it. I would call it the shell that surrounds an expressive aggregation, as Italians often are.
