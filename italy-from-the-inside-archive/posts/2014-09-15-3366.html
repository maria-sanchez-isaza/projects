A pleasure to look at

September 15, 2014 · Places

Yellow, blue, orange, green... These are the colors that have been used for the newly restored houses in the oldest neighborhoods of Trieste. Besides the architectural aspect, which makes them already a pleasure to look at, these bold colors are a statement of artistic bravery. Not to mention the entry doors... but that's another blog post.
