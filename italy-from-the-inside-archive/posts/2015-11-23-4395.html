La spaghettoteca

November 23, 2015 · Food

Remember this name, spaghettoteca, in case you are in Italy, you are hungry and you want to get a load of carbs. In a spaghettoteca there's not only spaghetti, but all sort of pasta. Lots of shapes, lots of sauces, lots of flavors.
