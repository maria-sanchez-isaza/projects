It was May 2014 when I first blogged about the basilica of Aquileia, but today's post will take you even deeper into what is considered to be one of the most beautiful Unesco's sites. This is exactly what you can experience with a new 3D technology called Matterport, that Paolo implemented during his last trip to Italy this past September and that allows you to generate 3D virtual tours.
