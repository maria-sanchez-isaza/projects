The Italian antipasto

November 21, 2017 · Food

The antipasto (appetizer) usually includes cold cuts such as salami, ham, mortadella along with cheese and pickles. But it could also consist of bruschetta, olives, prosciutto crudo (air-cured ham) with cantaloupe, and any other light food that can be eaten while waiting for the main courses.

Prosciutto crudo e melone (air-cured ham with cantaloupe)

Tuscan bruschette

Spiedini di mortadella, pomodorini e cetriolini (skewers of mortadella, cherry tomatoes and pickles)
