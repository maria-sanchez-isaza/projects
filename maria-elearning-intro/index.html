Why eLearning

Most of my experience has been on the technical side of XR, but my work has become increasingly focused on education. I’m interested in understanding how instructional design can help us use technology more intentionally. I see a lot of potential in the overlap between XR and eLearning, especially when immersive experiences are designed around learner needs, accessibility, and meaningful learning outcomes.
